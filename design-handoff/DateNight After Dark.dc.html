1 After Dark · couples-only category · one opt-in, Romantic and Spicy ideas together
Rules as designed. Onboarding asks who you're planning with first (1i); "Friends & family" users never see After Dark until they start a couple plan. After Dark exists only in 2-person groups and is on by default for couples: creating a couple plan shows it once (1a) with an 18+ confirmation and a clear "turn it off" option. The partner is asked once, on the first After Dark proposal they receive (1b), and says yes or not now. Either person can switch it off for themselves in Settings (1e) at any time. One switch, no levels: After Dark includes both Romantic and Spicy ideas, with an 18+ confirmation at opt-in. Romantic / Spicy remain as browse filters and labels only. Voting is per option with Yes / Maybe / Pass; a match needs two Yes. Your Passes are never shown to your partner, only matches and maybes (1d). Hidden from History always (your pick); discreet push text is available in Settings (1e), off by default. No Face ID or blur: neither store requires it, and it made the feature feel like something to hide. Content is a first-party seed catalog only (1g), suggestive not graphic, no third-party APIs, no photos in this category; After Dark Share Cards use type-only templates (1f). Rating: Romantic + Spicy as written is "frequent mature or suggestive content", so the app ships at 16+ on iOS and Mature 17+ on Google Play. Nothing explicit, so not 18+. User-written ideas stay private to the couple and get a report/block path so they don't raise the rating. It uses its own accent (wine) so it reads as a different room, not a different app.
