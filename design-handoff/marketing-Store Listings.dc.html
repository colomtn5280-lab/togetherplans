Description

47 messages later, still no plan? Together Plans ends the "what do you want to do?" loop.

How it works
1. Someone proposes 3–5 things to do
2. Everyone votes on their favourite
3. When you agree, it locks in. Calendar, done.

Made for couples, works for crews
Two people or twelve. Friday nights, Sunday afternoons, the family trip.

Never run out of ideas
Discover restaurants, films, hikes, galleries and nights out near you, and drop them straight into a proposal.

No match? Counter it
Take 2 keeps the good options and swaps the rest.

Make it official
Share Cards turn a decided plan into a card for iMessage, WhatsApp or your story. 36 designs.

Vote from a text
Friends can vote without installing anything.

Just the two of you
Couples get After Dark: a private category of romantic ideas, on only when you both say so.

Free. No ads. Your plans are yours.
