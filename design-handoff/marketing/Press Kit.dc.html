Why now

Group chats got the coordination job by default and are bad at it. Shared calendars record decisions but don't make them. Together Plans sits in between: a lightweight vote that ends in a calendar entry.

Who it's for

Couples first (one invite, one activation), then friend groups and families. Couples also get After Dark, a private category of romantic ideas that is only on when both people say so.

Launch release · week 9 · embargo 6am PT
Together Plans launches to end the "what do you want to do?" loop; friends can vote without installing the app

SAN FRANCISCO, [date] — TogetherPlans today released Together Plans, a free app for iOS, Android and the web that turns the planning thread into a three-step vote. Someone proposes three to five options, everyone votes, and when the group agrees the plan is locked in and added to the calendar.

Unlike shared calendars and group chats, Together Plans is built for the decision itself. People invited to a plan can vote from a text message using an App Clip or Google Play Instant experience without creating an account. When no one agrees, a counter-proposal keeps the popular options and replaces the rest.

"We counted. The average 'what should we do Friday' thread is 47 messages," said Jo [Surname], founder of TogetherPlans. "The average Together Plans decision is four minutes."

Decided plans can be turned into Share Cards, 36 designs sized for iMessage, WhatsApp and Instagram Stories. Couples also get After Dark, a private category of romantic ideas that is only active when both partners opt in.

Together Plans is free, has no advertising, and does not sell user data. It is available today in the US, UK, Canada and Australia. togetherplans.app
