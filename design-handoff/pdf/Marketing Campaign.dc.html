The hook
47 messages later, still no plan.
Propose three things. Everyone votes. It locks in. Plan together, go together.

1. Positioning

  • Audiences, in order: couples (primary), friend groups, families. Couples first because one invite equals one activation.
  • After Dark is a secondary hook in couples channels ("a private category for just the two of you"). Never in store screenshots 1–3, never in print. App rating 16+ (iOS), Mature 17+ (Play).
  • Voice: friendly, decisive, light. Specific beats clever. No couple stereotypes.

2. Loops before channels

Nothing in the channel list works unless these are healthy.

  1. Pair loop. A group is useless alone, so every activation is an invite. KPI: pair rate, the share of new users whose first invite is accepted within 48 hours. Target 45% at soft launch, 60% at full launch.
  2. Share Cards loop. Every decided plan can become a 9:16 card for iMessage, WhatsApp or Stories with a small "made with Together Plans" footer and a vote link. Target 0.4 cards per decided plan.
  3. Vote without install. iOS App Clip, Play Instant and a web vote page. Removes "my partner won't download an app". Highest-leverage engineering item in the plan.
  4. Screenshot loop. "47 messages later": users post their own worst planning thread next to the Together Plans result.
  5. Free web tool. togetherplans.app/decide, a no-signup picker. SEO magnet and a trial of the core mechanic.

8. Traditional, low-cost

  • Table cards at partner venues ("Can't decide? Scan."); the QR routes by device to the App Store, Play or /decide; the venue gets a Discover listing in return.
  • Stickers ("47 messages later", the calendar-heart) at climbing gyms, cinemas, farmers markets. $0.20 each.
  • Flyer for wedding fairs and vendor lobbies (the date-night registry).
  • Cinema pre-show slide at independents, $50–150 a month: "Deciding on the movie took longer than the movie."
  • Community and campus radio 15-second PSA-style read; free slots exist for local startups.
  • Postcards in partner venues' gift-card envelopes.

10. Measurement and rules

  • UTM on every link; one short domain (dtnt.app/…) with per-channel and per-venue slugs; QR codes generated per venue.
  • Stores: App Store custom product pages and Play custom listings per campaign ("couples", "friends", "wedding").
  • Events: invite_sent, invite_accepted, plan_decided, share_card_sent, card_link_opened, vote_without_install.
  • Weekly one-page dashboard: installs by source, pair rate, K-factor, CAC by channel, top three videos.
  • Reddit: contribute for three weeks before posting, disclose founder status, never astroturf.
  • After Dark never in ads that could reach under-18s; keep it out of the Play feature graphic and iOS screenshots 1–3.
  • Localisation before promoting a market: en-GB/en-AU spelling, date formats, local venue seed data.
