# DateMeUp — Keyword Research & ASO Strategy

**Audit Date:** October 2026  
**Product positioning:** Date night planning app for existing couples and groups — NOT a dating/matching app.

---

## ⚠️ Critical Framing: What DateMeUp Is (and Is Not)

**DateMeUp is:** A planning tool for people who are already in a relationship or friend group. They know who they're going with. They need help deciding *what to do* and *where to go*, and they want their partner/group to have input in the decision.

**DateMeUp is NOT:** A way to meet strangers, find a romantic partner, or connect with anyone new.

This distinction determines everything about keyword strategy:

| ❌ DO NOT TARGET | ✅ DO TARGET |
|------------------|--------------|
| dating app | date night planner |
| find a date | couples activity app |
| meet singles | what to do tonight with partner |
| dating site | date night ideas app |
| online dating | activity voting for couples |
| match with someone | plan date night together |

Targeting "dating app" keywords would drive the wrong user (single people) with high churn and likely negative reviews ("this isn't for meeting people"). The correct audience is couples who are already together and need to coordinate.

## Negative Keywords (Paid UA — Exclude These)

If DateMeUp ever runs Google UAC or Apple Search Ads, exclude:

```
dating app, dating site, find a date, meet singles, singles near me, 
hookup app, tinder alternative, bumble alternative, hinge alternative,
online dating, match.com, okcupid, dating, meet people
```

These exclusions prevent budget waste and wrong-audience installs that drive up churn.
