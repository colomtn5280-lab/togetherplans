# DateMeUp — Content Marketing & Organic Acquisition Strategy
**October 2026**

---

## Should DateMeUp Have a Content Section?

**Yes — but focused and lean, not a content farm.**

DateMeUp is competing for an underserved keyword space: **date night planning for established couples**. The major dating apps (Tinder, Bumble, Hinge) publish content about meeting people and first dates. Nobody owns the "you're already together — now what?" content space at scale.

This is the gap DateMeUp can exploit.

---

## Content Quality Standards

1. **Minimum 1,000 words** — Thin content ranks poorly and offers no real value
2. **Original insight** — Don't restate the same "try cooking together" lists that every content farm publishes. DateMeUp has unique activity data. Use it.
3. **Real activity examples** — Pull from the Discover database: specific venues, events, categories
4. **Conversion path on every page** — Every piece of content must have a clear next step to the app
5. **No AI content farms** — Do not mass-produce AI-generated articles. Google's Helpful Content system deprioritizes this. 10 excellent articles outperform 100 thin ones.
6. **Internal linking** — Every article links to 2-3 related articles + the homepage
7. **Updated annually** — "2026" in titles must be updated. Set calendar reminders.

## Organic Acquisition Funnel

```
Search: "date night ideas" → Blog article
→ Read article with real activity examples
→ "Plan this with DateMeUp" CTA
→ /date-night-planner landing page
→ Sign up or download app
→ Create first plan
→ Invite partner → Viral loop begins
```

The guest voting feature is the key viral loop: one user signs up, invites a partner to vote via link, partner experiences the app without installing, partner installs after seeing the value.
