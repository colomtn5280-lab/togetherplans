## Content Notes & Brand Voice

- **Tone:** Warm, slightly wry, practical. Not preachy. Humor earns its keep.
- **App mentions:** Keep organic. Never open with DateNight. Close with it naturally as a solution.
- **Avoid:** Listicle padding, generic relationship advice, anything that sounds like a press release.
- **Format:** All articles use proper Markdown (H1, H2, H3, bold, tables, bullet/numbered lists). CTA at the end links to `https://togetherplans.app`.
- **Internal linking:** Future articles should link to each other where relevant (e.g., the "cheap date ideas" article links to "50 date night ideas").
