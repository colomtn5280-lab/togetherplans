## TEMPLATE 1: Instagram DM — Short, Casual (for micro-influencers 10K–100K)

**Where to send:** Instagram DM to lifestyle/couple/city guide creators
**Length:** ~80 words — short enough to read on mobile in 10 seconds
**Tone:** Genuine, fan-forward, non-salesy

---

**[MESSAGE — copy and paste exactly]:**

> Hey [CREATOR'S FIRST NAME]! 👋 Love your content — especially [MENTION A SPECIFIC RECENT POST OR SERIES].
>
> I built an app called DateNight that helps couples and friend groups stop arguing about what to do — everyone votes on activity options and the app picks automatically. Honestly made it because we had this argument every week 😅
>
> Think it might actually be useful for you AND make fun content for your audience. Would you be open to trying it? No strings attached — just want honest thoughts!
>
> — Jake @ DateNight | togetherplans.app

---

**WHEN TO USE THIS:** First contact with any creator. DO NOT pitch compensation or collab specifics here — just open the conversation. Wait for their response before going further.

**PERSONALIZATION TIPS:**
- Replace [MENTION A SPECIFIC RECENT POST OR SERIES] with something REAL — scroll their feed first
- Don't change the emoji pattern — it tests well for DM response rates
- Add the specific post reference within the first line — proves you're not a bot

**FOLLOW-UP (if no response in 7 days):**
> "Hey [NAME] — just following up on my last message about DateNight! Totally understand if this isn't the right fit. If you want a free download code to try it, I'm happy to send one. No obligation either way. 💜"

## TEMPLATE 2: Email Pitch — Mid-Tier Lifestyle Creator (50K–500K followers)

**Where to send:** Email (find via link in bio, media kit page, or creator's management contact)
**Length:** ~250 words — long enough to be credible, short enough to finish
**Tone:** Professional but warm; founder-to-creator energy

---

**Subject line options (test both):**
- `"Quick pitch: app that could make great content for your audience (free + no obligation)"`
- `"DateNight app — think your followers would relate to this 👀"`

---

**[EMAIL — copy and paste, fill in brackets]:**

> Hi [CREATOR FIRST NAME],
>
> I'm Jake, founder of DateNight — an app I built after realizing my partner and I spent more time arguing about what to do on Friday nights than actually going out.
>
> Quick version: DateNight lets couples and friend groups propose activity options, vote democratically, and reach consensus automatically. No more 47-message group chats. No more "whatever you want." Just — a plan.
>
> I follow your content closely and loved [SPECIFIC POST/VIDEO] — your audience clearly deals with this same planning frustration, and I think DateNight would resonate genuinely.
>
> Here's what I'm proposing:
> - Free download + full access (no cost to you, ever)
> - If you like it and want to share it: one honest post/Story about your experience
> - If you don't like it: no obligation at all — honest feedback is equally valuable to us
>
> I'm not looking for a scripted ad. Just a real creator with a real audience trying a product that might actually help them.
>
> If you're open to trying DateNight, I'd love to send you a download link + a quick 3-minute walkthrough so you're not starting cold. Happy to answer any questions on a call or by email.
>
> Thank you for the content you make — it's great.
>
> Best,
> Jake
> Founder, TogetherPlans / DateNight
> hello@togetherplans.app
> togetherplans.app
> @togetherplans

---

**WHEN TO USE THIS:** After identifying a creator who regularly posts content about relationships, date nights, activities, or city guides and has engaged, genuine audiences.

**TIPS:**
- [SPECIFIC POST/VIDEO]: This is mandatory — do not skip it. Creators get hundreds of generic pitches. A genuine reference proves you watched their content.
- Do NOT attach anything to this email. Put everything in the body.
- Send Tuesday–Thursday, 9am–11am local time for best open rates.
- If they have a management contact (manager@ or press@), CC it but address the creator in the email.

**FOLLOW-UP (Day 7 if no response):**
> Subject: "Re: DateNight app"
> "Hi [NAME], just wanted to follow up on my note about DateNight. Completely understand if this isn't the right timing — no pressure at all. If you'd like a free download to try on your own terms, I'm happy to send one. Thanks for everything you create — genuinely a fan. — Jake"

## TEMPLATE 3: Podcast Guest Pitch Email

**Where to send:** Podcast host's email (usually in show notes or their website) or show's booking page
**Length:** ~200 words — hosts are busy; short wins
**Tone:** Confident, story-forward, interesting

---

**Subject line options:**
- `"Guest pitch: founder who built an app to stop couples from arguing (universal hook)"`
- `"App that ends 'what do you want to do?' — pitch for [SHOW NAME]"`

---

**[EMAIL — copy and paste, fill in brackets]:**

> Hi [HOST FIRST NAME],
>
> Big fan of [SHOW NAME] — [SPECIFIC EPISODE OR TOPIC] really resonated, especially [ONE SENTENCE ABOUT WHAT SPECIFICALLY RESONATED].
>
> I'm Jake, founder of DateNight (togetherplans.app) — an app I built to solve the "what do you want to do?" problem that every couple and friend group faces every single weekend. Turns out it's a $3B+ addressable market that nobody had specifically addressed.
>
> I think I'd make a great guest because:
> → The story is genuinely relatable (every listener has had this argument)
> → There's real psychology and behavioral design behind how we solve it
> → It's a launching consumer app with viral mechanics worth discussing
> → I can talk about building a product from a personal frustration — the "scratch your own itch" startup story
>
> Potential episode angles:
> - "The psychology of couple decision fatigue — and the app fixing it"
> - "Building a consumer app on zero budget: a founder story"
> - "Why 'what do you want to do?' is costing couples quality time"
>
> Happy to adjust to whatever angle works best for your audience.
>
> Available for a call anytime — you can reach me at hello@togetherplans.app.
>
> Best,
> Jake
> Founder, DateNight / TogetherPlans
> togetherplans.app | @togetherplans

---

**WHEN TO USE THIS:** Pitching any podcast that covers relationships, apps, startup stories, lifestyle, or personal productivity.

**WHICH SHOWS TO PRIORITIZE:**
1. Where Should We Begin (Esther Perel)
2. The Relationship School Podcast
3. My First Million
4. The Skinny Confidential Him & Her
5. How I Built This (NPR)
6. Your local city's lifestyle or startup podcast

**TIPS:**
- Customize [SPECIFIC EPISODE OR TOPIC] every single time — hosts verify this
- Keep the bullet-point sections; they're easy to skim
- If they have a formal booking form, still send the email AND fill the form
- Follow the host on social before emailing — like or reply to a recent post. Warm context helps.

**FOLLOW-UP (Day 10 if no response):**
> Subject: "Re: Guest pitch for [SHOW NAME]"
> "Hi [NAME], following up on my guest pitch. Totally understand if timing or topic isn't right — just wanted to make sure this didn't get buried. Happy to adjust the angle or provide more context. Thanks for all the great episodes. — Jake"

## TEMPLATE 4: Local Business Partnership Pitch

**Where to send:** Email to the owner or manager of local restaurants, escape rooms, bowling alleys, paint-and-sip studios, mini golf venues, comedy clubs, etc.
**Length:** ~180 words — business owners are very busy
**Tone:** Direct, mutually beneficial, no fluff

---

**Subject line options:**
- `"Free listing + customer referrals from DateNight app — partnership inquiry"`
- `"Local app wants to send customers your way — quick note"`

---

**[EMAIL — copy and paste, fill in brackets]:**

> Hi [OWNER/MANAGER NAME],
>
> I'm Jake, founder of DateNight — a free app (togetherplans.app) that helps couples and friend groups decide what to do together. We just launched in [CITY] and are building a local activity directory so that when users vote on "what to do tonight," local venues like [VENUE NAME] come up as top suggestions.
>
> I'd love to feature [VENUE NAME] in the app for free — no cost to you, ever. When DateNight users in [CITY] are planning an outing, your venue appears as a curated recommendation.
>
> In exchange, if you're open to it: we'd love to leave a small card or flyer near your [HOST STAND / FRONT DESK / TABLE], so guests who are already enjoying your venue can discover DateNight for their next outing.
>
> It's a zero-cost, zero-risk partnership that gets your business in front of an audience actively planning activities in [CITY].
>
> Would you be open to a quick 10-minute conversation or email exchange to set this up?
>
> Best,
> Jake
> Founder, DateNight | togetherplans.app
> hello@togetherplans.app | [PHONE NUMBER OPTIONAL]

---

**WHEN TO USE THIS:** Any local venue that caters to couples, groups, or people looking for activity experiences.

**TOP VENUE TARGETS:**
- Escape rooms (perfect group activity audience)
- Mini golf / bowling
- Paint and sip studios
- Comedy clubs
- Rooftop bars/restaurants
- Rock climbing gyms
- Axe throwing venues
- Movie theaters
- Drive-ins

**IN-PERSON VERSION (if you visit):**
> "Hi! I'm Jake — I built an app called DateNight that helps couples and groups decide what to do in [CITY]. I'd love to feature [Venue] in the app — totally free — and drop off some cards for your customers. Do you have 5 minutes to chat or a card I can email?"

**TIPS:**
- Call ahead before dropping in — ask who handles marketing or partnerships
- Bring printed business cards with QR codes when visiting in person
- Offer to print and provide the flyers yourself (removes their effort barrier entirely)
- Follow up once by email; if no response after second follow-up, move to next venue
