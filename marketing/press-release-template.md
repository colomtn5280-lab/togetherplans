## New App Ends the Relationship's Most Annoying Argument — "What Do You Want To Do Tonight?"

### DateNight Launches Free iOS and Android App That Turns Couple and Group Indecision Into Instant Consensus

---

**[CITY, STATE] — [DATE]** — Couples and friend groups worldwide know the loop well: "What do you want to do?" "I don't know, what do YOU want to do?" For many, this exchange repeats every weekend — ending in Netflix by default and a quiet sense of opportunity lost. Today, TogetherPlans launches **DateNight**, a free iOS and Android app that turns group activity indecision into instant, democratic consensus.

DateNight allows users to create a planning session, propose activity options, invite their partner or group via any of five methods — link, email, SMS, QR code, or username — and let everyone vote. The app's consensus engine automatically surfaces the top-ranked choice, eliminating debate and replacing it with a plan everyone actually agreed to.

"It wasn't a lack of things to do — our city is full of options. It was the decision overhead. Nobody wants to 'be the one who decides' and have it be the wrong call. ---

### The Problem: A Universal, Unaddressed Pain Point

Decision fatigue is a documented psychological phenomenon — by Friday evening, most adults have depleted their capacity for high-stakes decision-making after a full work week. Yet the unstructured "what do you want to do?" conversation demands exactly that kind of effortful, open-ended reasoning from both parties simultaneously.

The result: an estimated 40% of planned date nights never materialize, not because couples don't want to go out, but because the planning conversation becomes a barrier rather than a bridge. Group activity planning faces an even more complex version of the same problem: with four to eight people, preference heterogeneity and social dynamics make any single person's recommendation feel presumptuous.

DateNight solves this with a voting structure that removes social pressure: everyone submits their preferences independently, and the consensus reflects the group's genuine will rather than whoever spoke loudest.

### Key Features

- **Group Planning Sessions** — Create a named plan with a theme, date, and location for any size group
- **Activity Proposals** — Add multiple activity options with descriptions, photos, and links
- **Five Invite Methods** — Invite anyone via shareable link, email, SMS, QR code, or @username
- **Consensus Voting Engine** — Every member votes; the app calculates and displays the group consensus automatically
- **Location-Based Discovery** — Browse curated local activity suggestions based on the group's location
- **Multi-Group Support** — Create separate plans for couples, friend groups, family outings, and work events simultaneously
