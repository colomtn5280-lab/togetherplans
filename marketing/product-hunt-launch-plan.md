## THE PRODUCT HUNT OPPORTUNITY

A strong Product Hunt launch can deliver:
- 500–2,000+ downloads in 24 hours
- Top-tier backlinks (DA 90+) to your website
- TechCrunch and press pickup (reporters watch PH daily)
- "As seen on Product Hunt" credibility badge forever
- Early adopter community of tech-savvy power users
- Connections with other founders and potential investors

Product Hunt resets at **12:01am Pacific Time** every day. Products launched on that day compete for "Product of the Day," "Week," and "Month" honors. Tuesday and Wednesday launches get the most traffic. Avoid Mondays (competitive) and weekends (less traffic).

**Cost: $0. Commitment: ~2 weeks of preparation + 1 all-hands launch day.**

## PHASE 1: PRE-LAUNCH (2 Weeks Before Launch Day)

### Week -2 (14 Days Before)

**Day -14: Set Up Your Product Hunt Profile**
- [ ] Create your personal Product Hunt account at producthunt.com (if you don't have one)
- [ ] Complete your profile: photo, bio, Twitter/X link, website
- [ ] Start engaging with PH: upvote products you genuinely like, leave thoughtful comments
- [ ] Goal: Build a profile with activity before your launch day (looks authentic vs. brand-new day-of)

**Day -14: Find a Hunter**
A "hunter" submits your product on your behalf. A well-known hunter can bring their follower notification list to your launch (free boost of upvotes).

**How to find a hunter:**
1. Go to producthunt.com/leaderboard/hunters — browse top hunters
2. Look for hunters who have hunted similar consumer/lifestyle apps
3. Check their Twitter/X (most are active there)
4. Send a genuine, short DM:

> "Hey [NAME]! Big fan of the products you've hunted — especially [SPECIFIC PRODUCT]. I'm launching DateNight next week — a free app that ends the 'what do you want to do?' debate for couples and groups (togetherplans.app). Would you be open to being the hunter? Happy to share the full product details and assets. Thanks!"

**Backup plan:** If no hunter responds, you can self-submit. Self-submitted products still rank well if your upvote game is strong.

**Day -13: Prepare All Product Hunt Assets**

Required:
- [ ] **Product Name:** "DateNight by TogetherPlans"
- [ ] **Tagline (60 chars max):** `Stop debating. Start doing. Plan dates together.`
  - Alternative: `The app that ends "what do you want to do?" forever`
- [ ] **Description (260 chars max):** `DateNight helps couples and friend groups stop arguing about what to do. Propose activities, vote together, and get instant consensus. 5 invite methods. Free on iOS + Android.`
- [ ] **Thumbnail:** 240x240px square — app icon or branded illustration
- [ ] **Gallery images (up to 5):** App screenshots with headline overlays (see marketing report Section 9 for specs)
- [ ] **Video (optional but recommended):** Your "DateNight in 60 Seconds" demo video (see video-scripts.md)
- [ ] **First comment:** Prepared in advance (see Phase 2 below)
- [ ] **Maker comment:** Jake's personal "why I built this" story (pre-written, see below)

**Day -12: Build Your Notify List**
Product Hunt has a "Notify me on launch" feature. Get people to sign up BEFORE launch day.

- Share your Product Hunt "coming soon" page on:
  - Twitter/X: "We're launching on Product Hunt next week — click to get notified 👇 [link]"
  - Instagram Stories: "Big week coming — we're launching on Product Hunt! Tap to get notified."
  - LinkedIn (personal account): "I'm launching DateNight on Product Hunt — support would mean everything 🙏 [link]"
  - Direct text to close friends/family: "Hey — can you click this and hit 'notify me'? Launching next week. Means a lot."

Goal: 50+ pre-launch notification signups

**Day -10: Prepare Your Upvote Outreach List**
Create a spreadsheet of every person you'll personally contact on launch day:
- Personal contacts (family, friends, college friends, coworkers, ex-colleagues)
- Professional contacts (investors, advisors, other founders)
- Online community members who know you (Twitter followers, Reddit friends, Discord members)
- Anyone who has expressed interest in the app

Target list: 100–200 people minimum.

For each person, draft your personalized message (see Phase 2 — launch day outreach templates below).

**Day -8: Draft All Launch Day Content**
Prepare the following in a Google Doc, ready to copy-paste on launch day:
- [ ] First comment (founder story) — see below
- [ ] 10 responses to common questions (anticipate what PH community will ask)
- [ ] Twitter announcement thread (see below)
- [ ] Email to your mailing list
- [ ] Instagram Story sequence
- [ ] TikTok video or Story

**Day -7: Brief Your Inner Circle**
Contact your 20 closest supporters directly:
> "Hey [NAME] — I'm launching DateNight on Product Hunt next [DAY]. I'm going to need your help between 12:01am and 5pm PST on that day. It's just one click — upvote the product. Can I count on you? I'll text you the link the morning of."

This personal ask has a much higher conversion rate than a broadcast message.

**Day -5: Set Up Tracking**
- Create a UTM link for your Product Hunt launch:
  `togetherplans.app?utm_source=producthunt&utm_medium=launch&utm_campaign=ph_launch_day1`
- Connect Google Analytics to track PH traffic separately
- Set up App Store Connect and Google Play Console dashboards to monitor download spikes

**Day -2: Final Prep**
- [ ] All assets uploaded and saved in PH draft (don't submit yet)
- [ ] Outreach messages written and ready to send
- [ ] Team / friends briefed and ready
- [ ] Social posts scheduled or ready to post manually
- [ ] Set alarm for 11:55pm Pacific the night before launch

## COMMENT STRATEGY: How to Respond to Product Hunt Comments

**Respond to every comment. No exceptions. Do it within 30 minutes.**

**Common questions and suggested responses:**

**Q: "How is this different from just a group chat poll?"**
> A: "Great question! The main differences: (1) The consensus engine ranks everyone's preferences mathematically vs. a simple majority vote — so it finds what the GROUP likes, not just what's most popular. (2) The activity discovery layer surfaces local options they might not have considered. (3) It reduces the social pressure of being the one who 'decides' — everyone votes privately. The group chat poll still has the meta-problem of 'who creates the poll and chooses the options.' We handle that too."

**Q: "Why wouldn't people just use Google or Yelp?"**
> A: "Both tools give you options — DateNight gives you a decision. Yelp is great for finding a specific restaurant once you've decided to go to a restaurant. But that first step — 'do we want dinner, or mini golf, or an escape room?' — that's the actual friction point. We start there."

**Q: "Is there a paid tier?"**
> A: "Right now it's completely free for all features. We'll eventually offer a premium tier with advanced features — but the core consensus mechanic will always be free. We want everyone to be able to stop arguing. 😄"

**Q: "What about privacy? Does the app see my location?"**
> A: "Location is only used for the activity discovery feature, only when you request local suggestions, and you can decline entirely. We take privacy seriously — full policy at api.togetherplans.app/privacy. No data sold, ever."

## KEY PRODUCT HUNT RULES

1. **You CANNOT ask people to upvote** on Product Hunt's platform itself (in comments). You CAN ask via external channels (text, email, DM, social).

2. **Upvotes from new accounts with no history may be discounted** by PH's algorithm. That's okay — focus on real contacts with existing PH accounts.

3. **Comments matter as much as upvotes** for ranking. Substantive conversation on your product page signals genuine interest.

4. **Don't submit on weekends** — traffic is 40% lower and competition is unpredictable.

5. **The first 4 hours are critical** — early momentum affects your ranking algorithm position for the whole day. Your inner circle must move fast in the morning hours.

6. **Be genuine** — PH is a community. The makers who win are those who show up authentically, not those who game the system. Your real story (built this for my own couple frustration) is compelling. Use it.
