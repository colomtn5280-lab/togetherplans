**Caption:**
> why is planning a date night so hard? a thread on the psychology of decision fatigue (and how we fixed it) 🧵

> 1/ Decision fatigue is real. The average adult makes 35,000 decisions a day. By Friday night, you have almost nothing left.

> 2/ When both people say "whatever you want" — neither is being lazy. They're genuinely depleted. This is neuroscience.

> 3/ The fix isn't "try harder." It's removing the decision overhead entirely. Give people a vote instead of a blank page.

> 4/ That's the design principle behind DateNight. Voting is cognitively easier than generating options AND evaluating them.

> 5/ Propose options → vote → consensus. Three steps. 90 seconds. Full evening freed.
