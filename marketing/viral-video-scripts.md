## VIDEO SCRIPT 1: "The Loop"
**Concept:** The universal "what do you want to do?" conversation acted out perfectly, ending with DateNight as the resolution. Relatable, funny, shareable.
**Length:** 45 seconds
**Format:** Comedy skit — can be filmed alone (split-screen) or with a partner
**Equipment needed:** iPhone, stable surface or tripod, natural light

---

### SCENE BREAKDOWN

**[SCENE 1 — 0:00 to 0:05]**
*Setting: A couch or bed. One or two people visible. Friday night vibe — dim light, casual clothes.*

**[On-screen text overlay]:** "Us. Every. Friday."
**[Music]:** Silence, or subtle ambient sound

**Person 1 (or left side of split screen):**
> "What do you want to do tonight?"

**Person 2 (right side of split screen / same person cuts to reaction):**
> "I don't know... what do YOU want to do?"

---

**[SCENE 2 — 0:05 to 0:15]**
*Same setting, now both look slightly more tired/frustrated.*

**[On-screen text overlay]:** "10 minutes later..."

**Person 1:**
> "Okay what about— [pause] —no, never mind."

**Person 2:**
> "What? No, say it."

**Person 1:**
> "I was gonna say sushi but you always say you're not that hungry."

**Person 2:**
> "I LIKE sushi."

**Person 1:**
> "Since WHEN?"

---

**[SCENE 3 — 0:15 to 0:22]**
*Clock on wall visible. Jump cut. Both look defeated.*

**[On-screen text overlay]:** "45 minutes later..."
**[On-screen text]:** [Netflix logo or generic streaming icon appearing] "...again."

*Both slumped. Nothing happened.*

---

**[SCENE 4 — 0:22 to 0:38]**
*CUT TO: Clean, bright phone screen recording of DateNight*

**[Voiceover — Person 1, upbeat shift in tone]:**
> "Okay so I downloaded this app called DateNight. Watch."

*[Screen shows: Create plan → Add three options: Sushi, Mini Golf, Movie → Invite partner → Partner votes → Consensus: 🎳 Mini Golf — 2/2 votes → "Let's go!" screen]*

**[On-screen text overlay]:** "Two votes. Ninety seconds. Done."

---

**[SCENE 5 — 0:38 to 0:45]**
*CUT TO: Couple at mini golf, genuinely laughing. Shot on phone, authentic.*

**[On-screen text overlay]:** "DateNight. Stop debating. Start doing."
**[Voiceover]:** "Download free. Link in bio."
**[Final frame]:** DateNight logo + togetherplans.app

---

### FILMING NOTES
- Film Scene 1–3 together with minimal cuts; the monotony IS the joke
- Use natural light from a window — don't over-light this
- Exaggerate the exhausted reactions slightly for comedy
- Scene 5 can be any actual activity — film it in real life for authenticity
- For solo creator: use split-screen effect (available in CapCut) to play both characters

### RECOMMENDED MUSIC/AUDIO
- Scenes 1–3: Deadpan silence OR the classic "sad trombone" SFX
- Scene 4: Upbeat, satisfying "solution" sound — like a lightbulb ding
- Scene 5: Something warm and upbeat — trending TikTok audio that fits a "happy ending"

**Length:** 60 seconds (edit of longer content)
**Format:** Vlog / first-person / real evening
**Equipment needed:** iPhone on stabilizer or handheld, front and back camera

---

- The more REAL this feels, the better it performs. Don't over-edit.

- The partner MUST appear willing and genuine. Forced = bad content.
- This is the format that can also be a 5–7 minute YouTube video with the full night shown

### RECOMMENDED MUSIC/AUDIO
- Vlog-style background music throughout (LoFi, upbeat indie)
- Audio from the actual evening should come through naturally — keep real ambient sounds
- End on an upbeat track for the montage

## VIDEO SCRIPT 4: "Types of People in Your Group"
**Concept:** Fast-paced comedy sketch identifying the 4 personality types in every group that can't agree — ending with DateNight as the universal solution. Highly shareable, tag-able.
**Length:** 45 seconds
**Format:** Comedy character sketch — works solo (quick costume/expression changes) or with a group
**Equipment needed:** iPhone, stable surface, good light — ideally consistent setting for all characters

---

### SCENE BREAKDOWN

**[OPENING — 0:00 to 0:03]**
*Creator on camera, addressing audience directly.*

**[On-screen text overlay]:** "Every friend group has these people 👇"

**Creator:**
> "Every group chat. Every time. Let me show you."

---

**[CHARACTER 1 — 0:03 to 0:10]**
*Quick cut. Same person, or a different person. Slightly more energetic expression.*

**[On-screen text overlay, big + bold]:** "THE SUGGESTER 💡"

**The Suggester (enthusiastically):**
> "Okay okay okay — what about escape room? Or paint night? Or we could do the rooftop thing, or there's that new bowling place, or—"

**[Cut to rest of group staring, unresponsive]**

**The Suggester:**
> "...what."

---

**[CHARACTER 2 — 0:10 to 0:18]**
*Different person or quick change. Arms crossed, slightly judgmental.*

**[On-screen text overlay]:** "THE VETOER ❌"

**The Vetoer:**
> "Not escape room. We did that last month. Not paint night — too expensive. Not rooftop — I don't want to dress up. Not bowling—"

**[Character from Scene 1]:**
> "WHY NOT BOWLING?"

**The Vetoer:**
> "I just don't."

---

**[CHARACTER 3 — 0:18 to 0:25]**
*Completely passive energy. Barely visible effort.*

**[On-screen text overlay]:** "THE 'WHATEVER' PERSON 🤷"

**Group member:**
> "What do YOU want to do?"

**The Whatever Person (immediately):**
> "Whatever you guys want!"

**[Beat. Group stares at each other.]**

**Someone:**
> "Do you actually not care or—"

**The Whatever Person (still totally flat):**
> "I literally do not mind at all. Anything is fine."

*[Long pause.]*

**The Whatever Person:**
> "...but NOT that new sushi place."

---

**[CHARACTER 4 — 0:25 to 0:33]**
*Creator on camera, completely calm, holding phone.*

**[On-screen text overlay]:** "THE ONE WHO DOWNLOADS DATENIGHT 📲 ← be this person"

**Creator (calmly):**
> "Everyone, I've created a plan. Three activity options. Vote by tonight."

*[Phones ding. Everyone votes.]*

**Creator:**
> "Consensus: bowling. We're going at 8."

*[The Vetoer and the Whatever Person look mildly surprised.]*

**The Vetoer (quietly):**
> "...okay actually bowling is fine."

---

**[OUTRO — 0:33 to 0:45]**
*Creator back to direct address.*

**Creator:**
> "Be the person who downloads DateNight. Your group will thank you. Link in bio."

**[On-screen text overlay]:**
> "DateNight — Free on iOS + Android"
> "Stop debating. Start doing."
> "togetherplans.app"

---

### FILMING NOTES
- For solo creator: Quick wardrobe/expression changes between characters (even just a hat/jacket swap = clear visual differentiation)
- For group: Recruit friends — more authentic, funnier dynamic
- The Vetoer's "I just don't" line should be very flat and unexplained — the comedy IS the lack of explanation
- The final "whatever person secretly has strong opinions" is the biggest laugh — pause after it
- Keep each character under 8 seconds — pace is everything

### RECOMMENDED MUSIC/AUDIO
- Upbeat, slightly comedic background music throughout — think "quirky sitcom underscore"
- Each character could have a tiny musical motif (1 note SFX) as their "on-screen text" appears
- End on the same upbeat music that plays throughout — resolution, not a new track

## QUICK REFERENCE: Production Checklist

**Before filming:**
- [ ] Script memorized (not read — it shows)
- [ ] Location scouted (clean background, no distractions)
- [ ] Natural light source identified (window or outdoor)
- [ ] Phone fully charged
- [ ] Do Not Disturb enabled
- [ ] CapCut open and ready for edit

**During filming:**
- [ ] Film each scene 3x minimum
- [ ] Film both landscape (YouTube) AND portrait (TikTok/Reels) versions
- [ ] Record VO separately with earbuds in quiet space
- [ ] Capture reaction shots (they're often the best moments)

**After filming:**
- [ ] Edit in CapCut (free)
- [ ] Add text overlays matching brand colors
- [ ] Add captions/subtitles (85% of TikTok watched muted)
- [ ] Check that CTA is clear in final 5 seconds
- [ ] Export in highest quality available
