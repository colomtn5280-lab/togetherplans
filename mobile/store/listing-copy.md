## Description (~2500 chars)

Stop texting "I don't know, what do you want to do?"

DateMeUp turns the endless back-and-forth into a quick, fun decision. Browse real events and places near you, pick 2–5 options you'd actually enjoy, send them to your partner or group — everyone votes, and when the group agrees, it's a match.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Discover — browse real concerts, restaurants, comedy shows, trails, and more happening near you, with photos, ratings, prices, and dates.
2. Build a plan — add 2–5 options as you browse. One tap each.
3. Everyone votes — each person picks a favorite.
4. It's a match — when the group agrees, the plan is locked in, with the date, address, and ticket link right there.

ANYONE CAN VOTE — NO ACCOUNT NEEDED

Send a vote link to anyone by text or WhatsApp. They tap their favorite in the browser — no download, no sign-up. Their vote shows up in your tally instantly.

REAL EVENTS, NOT GENERIC IDEAS

Suggestions come from live local data: concerts and shows with real dates and ticket links, restaurants with ratings and price levels, outdoor spots, volunteering opportunities, and more — across 15 categories from Restaurants & Dining to Comedy to Hiking & Trails.

TONIGHT NEAR ME

Free this evening? The Tonight banner surfaces what's actually happening near you right now — and falls back to the weekend if tonight is quiet.

PLAN A DAY TRIP

Widen your search radius up to 150 miles, or search another area entirely. Getaway planning works the same way: browse, propose, vote, go.

MORE WAYS TO PLAN TOGETHER

- Groups for couples, friends, or family
- Counter-proposals when someone has a better idea
- Nudge members who haven't voted yet
- Add the winning plan to your calendar in one tap
- Share a celebration card when it's a match
- After Dark: a late-night mode for couples (evenings only)

YOUR PRIVACY

No ads. We don't sell your data. Delete your account anytime.

DateMeUp makes "what do you want to do?" the easiest question of the week. Download it and plan your next night out together.

## What's New (v1.2.0)

DateMeUp 1.2.0 is a major update to how you discover and decide:

• Guest voting — send a vote link to anyone; they vote from their browser, no account or download needed
• Tonight near me — a new banner surfaces events happening tonight, with a weekend fallback when tonight is quiet
• Real event data everywhere — concerts, comedy, family events, wellness, and volunteering now show live local results with photos, dates, ratings, and ticket links
• 15 refreshed categories, including the new Sports & Active (games to watch, games to play)
• Day-trip planning — widen your search radius up to 150 miles or search another area by zip code
• Plan builder — collect options while you browse and finalize the proposal when you're ready
• Smarter notifications — a heads-up when you're the last vote missing, a reminder the day before a decided plan, and a ping when a guest votes on your proposal
• Solo start — begin a plan before you've created a group; we set one up as you go
• Vote carryover — guest votes convert to member votes when someone signs up
• Many fixes and polish throughout
