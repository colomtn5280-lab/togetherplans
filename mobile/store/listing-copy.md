## Full Description (4000 chars max — used for both stores)

Stop asking "What do you want to do?" and start actually doing it.

DateNight is the collaborative planning app that makes it easy for couples and groups to agree on what to do — without the endless back-and-forth texts.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Create a group — date night, friend group, family outing
2. Propose activities — suggest 3–5 ideas with details like cost, location, and time
3. Everyone votes — each person picks their favorite
4. Consensus reached — DateNight tells you what the group agreed on
5. Make it happen — stop debating, start going

FEATURES

Collaborative Planning
- Create shared groups for couples, friend groups, or families
- Propose multiple activity options in a single plan
- Everyone votes on their favorite — no one gets left out

Smart Activity Suggestions
- Browse curated local activity ideas by category
- Suggestions include dining, entertainment, outdoors, arts, nightlife, and more
- Tap to add any suggestion to your proposal instantly

5 Ways to Invite People
- Shareable link (copy & paste anywhere)
- Email invitation (sent directly from the app)
- SMS invitation (share via text)
- QR code (scan to join)
- Username (find friends by name)

Counter-Proposals
- Don't like the options? Propose an alternative
- Groups can negotiate until everyone's happy

Your Privacy, Protected
- No advertising. We never sell your data.
- Delete your account anytime — data gone within 30 days
- End-to-end encrypted communication

PERFECT FOR
- Couples planning date nights
- Friend groups deciding on activities
- Families coordinating outings
- Office teams planning events

DateNight makes the "what do you want to do?" problem disappear. Download it and plan your next adventure together.
